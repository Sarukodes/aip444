# Job Market Analysis: Software Development and Related Technical Roles

This report summarizes 10 job postings analyzed in Phase 1. Most of the postings are related to software development, AI/ML, cloud technologies, technical consulting, and digital analytics.

The analysis looks at common technical skills, experience and education requirements, salaries, responsibilities, industry trends, and company culture expectations.

## Common Required Skills

The most frequently identified required skills across the 10 postings were:

| Skill | Number of Postings |
| :--- | :---: |
| SQL | 4 |
| Python | 3 |
| API development | 2 |
| DevOps | 2 |
| Azure | 2 |
| Git | 2 |

SQL was the most common required skill in the analyzed postings, followed by Python.

API development, DevOps, Azure, and Git also appeared across multiple roles. This shows that employers are looking for candidates who can work with databases, backend development, cloud environments, version control, and modern software delivery practices.

## Common Preferred Skills

The most common preferred skills identified were:

| Skill | Number of Postings |
| :--- | :---: |
| AI | 2 |
| Cloud | 2 |

AI and cloud knowledge appeared as preferred skills in multiple postings. They were not required across the majority of the dataset, but they show that employers value familiarity with these areas.

## Experience Requirements

Most of the postings were aimed at mid-level, senior, or specialized candidates.

The experience requirements included:

- 4–7+ years for the Digital Agriculture Analytics Lead role
- 5+ years for the Java Backend Developer role
- Junior level for the Canonical Software Developer role
- 5+ years for the Senior Back-End .NET role
- 5 years or more for the Machine Learning Specialist role
- 3+ years of leadership/practice experience for the Forward Deployment Engineer Practice Manager role
- Senior level for the Source Code Software Developer role
- Senior level for the Maarut Full Stack Developer role
- 8 years for the SAP Fioneer FPSL Consultant role
- 3 years to less than 5 years for the Avant Software Developer role

Only one posting was explicitly targeted at a junior candidate. The rest of the dataset mainly focused on experienced or senior candidates.

This suggests that professional experience is an important requirement in this group of postings, especially for specialized AI, backend, consulting, and leadership roles.

## Education Requirements

Education requirements varied between the postings.

Four postings explicitly listed a Bachelor's degree or equivalent:

- Digital Agriculture Analytics Lead — Bachelor's degree in a relevant field or equivalent practical experience
- Junior Software Developer - Observability — Bachelor's or equivalent in Computer Science, STEM, or a similar degree
- Practice Manager, Forward Deployment Engineer — Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline
- Software Developer at Avant Techno Solutions — Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience

The Machine Learning Specialist role at Vector Institute required an earned doctorate degree.

The other postings did not specify an education requirement in the extracted data.

Overall, a Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience was the most common stated education requirement, while highly specialized research-oriented roles could require advanced education.

## Common Responsibilities

Several responsibilities appeared repeatedly across the analyzed roles:

- Designing, developing, and maintaining software applications and systems
- Building and managing APIs and integrations
- Writing, testing, debugging, and maintaining code
- Working with databases and data systems
- Collaborating with cross-functional teams
- Using cloud platforms and DevOps practices
- Troubleshooting and resolving technical problems
- Creating technical documentation
- Supporting security, governance, and risk-management requirements
- Testing and validating software solutions

Senior roles also included responsibilities such as mentoring developers, technical leadership, architecture decisions, stakeholder communication, and ownership of larger systems or projects.

## AI and Machine Learning Trends

AI and machine learning appeared across several different types of roles instead of only appearing in dedicated machine learning positions.

Examples from the dataset include:

- Machine Learning Specialist
- AI-enabled analytics in agriculture
- AI-assisted software engineering
- Responsible AI
- AI/ML development and architecture
- Forward-deployed AI engineering

This suggests that AI knowledge is becoming useful outside of dedicated AI jobs and is being included in software development, analytics, consulting, and leadership roles.

## Cloud and DevOps Trends

Cloud and DevOps technologies appeared across multiple postings.

Technologies and practices found in the extracted data included:

These skills were not present in every posting, but they appeared often enough to show that cloud deployment and modern development workflows are useful skills for this market.

## Industry Trends

The postings came from several industries and types of organizations, including:

- software and technology
- financial services
- IT services and consulting
- AI and machine learning research
- food and beverage manufacturing
- digital agriculture

One noticeable pattern was the use of software, analytics, and AI skills across industries that are not traditionally considered software companies.

For example, McCain Foods had roles involving analytics, AI transformation, and forward-deployed engineering.

This shows that software and AI skills are being used across a wider range of industries.

## Company Culture Expectations

The company research showed several recurring culture signals:

- collaboration with cross-functional teams
- continuous learning and professional development
- problem-solving
- technical ownership
- communication with different stakeholders
- inclusive and supportive work environments
- flexibility in some workplaces
- responsible use of technology

Some senior postings also emphasized mentoring, leadership, documentation, knowledge sharing, and helping other team members improve.

## Notable Trends

### 1. AI is becoming part of general software work

AI appeared not only in dedicated machine learning jobs but also in backend development, analytics, consulting, and leadership roles.

### 2. SQL and Python are useful across different roles

SQL was the most frequently identified required skill, while Python was also common. These skills appeared in different types of technical positions rather than only one specific job category.

### 3. Experienced candidates are heavily represented

Only one of the 10 postings was explicitly junior. Most of the other jobs required several years of experience or were described as senior or specialized roles.

### 4. Cloud and DevOps knowledge adds value

Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Git, and other cloud/DevOps technologies appeared across multiple postings.

### 5. Responsible AI and security are becoming more important

Some roles specifically mentioned Responsible AI, governance, security, privacy, or risk management. This suggests employers are considering not only how AI and software are built, but also how they are used safely.

### 6. Technical skills are needed across different industries

The dataset includes organizations in technology, finance, agriculture, manufacturing, consulting, and AI research. Technical skills such as software development, APIs, cloud computing, analytics, and AI are useful across all of these areas.

## Overall Summary

Based on these 10 postings, the strongest recurring technical skills were SQL and Python, followed by skills related to APIs, DevOps, Azure, and Git.

The dataset mainly targeted experienced candidates, although there was also one junior opportunity. Bachelor's degrees or equivalent experience appeared in several postings, while a specialized machine learning role required a doctorate.

AI/ML, cloud technologies, APIs, DevOps practices, and secure software development appeared across different industries. Employers also commonly expected collaboration, problem-solving, communication, and continuous learning.

For someone preparing for similar software and AI-related roles, the market data suggests that strengthening backend development, SQL, Python, cloud/DevOps skills, API development, and practical AI knowledge would be useful areas to focus on.
